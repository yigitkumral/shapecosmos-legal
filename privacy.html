Shape Cosmos: Arrow Escape 3D — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

This policy explains the use of personal data in Shape Cosmos: Arrow Escape 3D.

1. Who is responsible?

The data controller and publisher is Yiğit Kumral, İzmir, Türkiye. Contact: yigitkumral@icloud.com.

2. Which data do we use and why?

Data is collected automatically through the app and service providers' software, or from information you send us.

  • Account and gameplay data: On first launch the app creates an anonymous guest account in Unity: random player and installation IDs. It is used to determine regional permission rules, provide online features and support progress. Your chosen username, social connections and race results are also tied to the account. Game saves and settings are stored on your device. Unity Gaming Services keeps account data, a short progress summary (current level, number of completed levels, app version, last-seen time) and support operation records; your full game save and moves are not sent. Your username, online status and relevant race results may be visible to players you interact with.
  • Connection and device data: IP address, approximate region, device/operating system and app information are processed to operate services, maintain security and determine regional permission rules. Region is determined from your IP on Unity's server; device language and precise location are not used.
  • Purchase data: Product and transaction IDs and purchase history are used to process purchases, verify delivery and prevent abuse. For these purposes Unity IAP sends purchase events to Unity with product, transaction and installation IDs and the device's identifier for this publisher (IDFV); this does not depend on the usage data switch. If usage data is on, the purchase event also goes to Unity Analytics. Apple handles payments; we do not receive card or bank details.
  • Usage data: Gameplay interactions, progress, duration and performance measurements are processed with player/installation IDs and device information in Unity Analytics to improve the app. We do not use this analytics data for advertising.
  • Advertising data: Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads may process device identifiers, approximate location, device information, ad interactions and purchase information for ad delivery, personalisation, measurement and fraud prevention. Personalisation is on only if you accept in the game's ad window and, on iPhone/iPad, Apple tracking permission is also granted; otherwise ads are shown without personalisation. The iOS advertising identifier is used only with Apple tracking permission.
  • Diagnostics and support: Error/crash logs and performance information are sent through Unity Diagnostics with Unity's installation ID, which counts as personal data. Crash reporting does not depend on the usage data switch and is not used for advertising. When you write to us, your email address, message and attachments are used only for support and bug fixing.
  • Test recording (TestFlight test version only): If you want, you can start a gameplay recording with the red record button; before every recording the game shows what is collected and asks for your consent. A recording holds the screen frames that change (at most one frame a second, not a video), your voice if you allow the microphone, game events and device/version details; without microphone permission it records silently. When it ends it is uploaded to Unity Cloud Save with your guest account's player ID; without internet it waits on your phone. Only the developer sees it, and it is used to improve the game. The store version has no recording, and test recordings are not carried over to it. If someone else is on screen or can be heard, get their consent too.
  • Preferences: Permission choices and change history are stored on the device; permission status is passed to relevant services to apply your choices.

3. Who receives data?

  • Unity Gaming Services: processes account and online gameplay data, progress summaries, regional configuration, analytics, diagnostics, purchase processing and test recordings to provide services. Unity privacy policy.
  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and Unity Ads: process data for advertising under their own policies. Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (covers LevelPlay and Unity Ads).
  • Apple: handles downloads, purchases and the tracking permission under its own policy. Apple privacy policy.

Other players see only the information described in section 2. Necessary data may be disclosed to authorities when legally required. We do not sell your data.

4. Legal basis

Accounts, purchases and requested online services are processed for performance of a contract; security, troubleshooting and support for legitimate interests; rights requests and mandatory records for legal obligations. Under GDPR these are Articles 6(1)(b), 6(1)(f) and 6(1)(c), respectively; under Türkiye's KVKK, Articles 5/2(c), 5/2(f) and 5/2(ç). Usage data where consent is requested, personalised ads and test recordings rely on consent (GDPR 6(1)(a), KVKK 5/1). Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

5. International transfers

Unity and Apple may process data outside Türkiye, including in the United States. Unity's data processing agreement includes standard contractual clauses for transfers from Europe. You may request copies of relevant safeguards and transfer information at our contact address.

6. How long do we keep data?

  • Local game saves are kept until the app or relevant data is deleted; account data, persistent online gameplay data, progress summaries and support operation records until account deletion. Temporary session data is deleted when the session closes.
  • Unity Analytics retains personal data for 13 months by default, and Diagnostics for 90 days. Derived analytics profiles may remain until deletion is requested.
  • Test recordings are kept for up to one year after upload; support correspondence for 12 months after the request closes. You may request earlier deletion.
  • Advertising, Unity purchase and Apple transaction data follow the providers' retention policies. Legally required records are retained only for the necessary scope and duration.

7. Your choices

The app asks for no permission when it opens. Your choices keep every feature of the game open; the ad reward is the same either way.

  • Usage data: In the European consent group (EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland and associated territories) and on devices whose region cannot be determined it starts off; if you have not been asked before, Settings opens when you leave the level 2 result and the choice is yours. Opening and closing Settings is not consent. In other regions (including Türkiye) it starts on. You can turn it off everywhere in Settings; turning it off stops sending, and an earlier refusal is kept.
  • Ad personalisation: In every country, your first rewarded-ad request opens a window with equally weighted Accept and Decline buttons. If you accept, on iPhone/iPad Apple asks for tracking permission if it has not asked before. Personalisation is on only when both permissions are given. If you decline, Apple is not asked and ads are shown without personalisation.
  • Changing it later: The Personalised ads switch in Settings shows the actual state and is independent of usage data. When you turn it on, Apple asks if it has never asked; if you refused before, the app's page in your phone's Settings opens. If the device restricts tracking, it cannot be turned on from the game. You can also change Apple's permission on your iPhone in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Crash reporting and purchase processing do not depend on these switches. A sale/sharing opt-out is sent to advertising services in every region.

8. Your rights and how to exercise them

Depending on applicable law, you may request information, access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability, object to processing, withdraw consent and complain to a data protection authority. Rights under KVKK Article 11 also include learning transfer recipients, notifying recipients of corrections/deletions, objecting to adverse outcomes from automated analysis and seeking compensation for damage.

Write to yigitkumral@icloud.com. We may request necessary information to match your account. We respond to KVKK requests within 30 days and GDPR requests within one month. If a GDPR extension is permitted, we explain the reason within the first month. You may also contact Unity directly about advertising data.

9. Deleting your account

While online, use Settings > Account > Delete account and confirm. Your account and associated online data (friends, friend code, race results on the server, progress summary and support operation records) and test recordings, both uploaded and still waiting on your phone, are deleted; a Unity Analytics data deletion request is sent. Without internet, or if an error occurs, nothing is deleted and you can try again. Your local game progress and settings remain. This does not refund purchases; refunds are requested through Apple. If you do not see the Account row, cannot access the app or have another deletion request, including diagnostics data, write to our contact address.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children; we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Parental consent is required below the age set by applicable law; in the EU this ranges from 13 to 16 by country. Contact us for deletion if you believe your child's data has been collected.

11. Security

Data is transmitted to services over encrypted connections; access controls help prevent unauthorised access. No system can guarantee absolute security.
