Shape Cosmos: Arrow Escape 3D — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

This text explains which data the game Shape Cosmos: Arrow Escape 3D ("the game") uses and why. You do not need to give your name, email address or phone number to play.

Summary

  • Your level progress, settings, powers and purchased packs are stored only on your device.
  • When you enter multiplayer races, the game creates an automatic guest account for you. The account holds a player ID, your username, your friend list and your race results. These are kept in Unity Gaming Services.
  • You can delete your account from inside the game at any time: Settings > Account > Delete account.
  • Ads are shown only when you ask for one (rewarded ads). Ads are served by Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads.
  • The game asks you nothing when it opens. Depending on your region, it asks for permissions at your first rewarded ad or at the end of level 2 (section 7).
  • In the European Union, the EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Türkiye (or if your device's region cannot be read), personalised ads and usage data start off. They turn on only if you allow them.
  • In other countries, usage data starts on and you can turn it off in Settings. On iOS, your advertising ID is shared only if you allow tracking.
  • Your permission answers never lock any feature; the ad reward is the same either way.
  • Apple handles purchases. Your payment details never reach us.
  • We do not sell your data.

1. Who is responsible?

The data controller is Yiğit Kumral (an individual, İzmir, Türkiye). The game is published by Yiğit Kumral. For any question or request: yigitkumral@icloud.com

2. Which data do we use and why?

  • Game data that stays on your device: Your level progress, an unfinished level, your power stock, your +2 life tokens, your purchase records and your settings (such as language and vibration) are kept in the app's folder on your device. These files are not sent to us or to any other server. If you delete the game, this data is deleted too and cannot be restored.
  • Guest account and player ID: The first time you use multiplayer, Unity Authentication gives you a random player ID (anonymous sign-in). This ID is needed for race matchmaking, friends and race results. Your device also keeps a randomly generated guest ID and a tag such as "Guest #1234".
  • Sign in with Apple: The game does not offer Sign in with Apple or account linking today. If it is added later, this policy will be updated first.
  • Username: If you want, you choose a name of 3–16 characters (letters, digits or underscore). If you do not, your guest tag is shown. Your name is shown to the players you race and to your friends. We recommend that you do not use your real name.
  • Friend code and friends: A six-digit friend code is tied to your account. A person you share the code with can add you as a friend. Your friend list, friend requests, room invites and whether you are online are kept in the Unity Friends service and are visible to your friends. Your code and which player it belongs to are kept in Unity Cloud Code and Cloud Save so that you can be added by code and the code stays unique; they are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Race data: During a race, your name, the levels you play, your progress, your mistakes and your time go to the other players in the same room through Unity Lobby. While a race is running your device writes a "still here" mark (a timestamp only) to the room every 15 seconds; if none arrives for more than a minute you count as having left the race. This lobby data is deleted when the room closes.
  • Race results: In friend room races, your own result (your name, player ID, time per level, mistakes and whether you finished) is written to Unity Cloud Save. This way everyone sees the same result table even if an app closes. The players you raced read this record; it is stored in Unity with public read access, and only you can change it. Results of quick 1v1 races are not written to the server.
  • Purchases: You buy the packs in the Sales Station through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; your card or payment details never reach us. The game keeps the product ID and transaction ID on your device to deliver the pack and to avoid granting the same order twice.
  • Ads and advertising ID: When you watch a rewarded ad, Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads collect data from your device to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud: device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. On iOS, the advertising ID (IDFA) is shared only if you allow tracking. In the consent regions (section 7), personalised ads are shown only if you allow them; if you do not, ads are shown without personalisation. These services are independent controllers of this data under their own policies.
  • Usage data: In the consent regions (section 7), usage data starts off; the game asks once at the end of level 2 and sends it only if you allow it. In other regions it starts on, and you can turn it off at any time with the Settings > Usage data switch. We send the following with Unity Analytics: the levels you start, finish or leave, time, taps and turns, lives lost, power use and the result of power ads, the tutorial cards you see, frame rate, device model and memory size. Unity Analytics adds a player or installation ID, device and operating system information, and the country derived from your IP address. We use this data only to improve the game; we do not use it for ads.
  • Crash reports: If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, a technical error report is sent through Unity's Diagnostics service so that we can find and fix the problem. The report contains technical details such as device model, operating system, app version and the error log, plus a random installation ID; it contains no personal information such as your name, email or account, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. This report does not depend on your consent and is sent in every region.
  • Test feedback (TestFlight test version only): In the game's TestFlight test version you can start a gameplay recording with the record button at the top right if you want; before every recording the game shows what is collected and where it goes and asks for your consent. The recording holds the screen frames that change (at most one frame a second, not a video), your voice from the microphone (if you allow the microphone), game events (such as taps and level starts and ends) and device and app version details. When it ends it is sent to Unity Gaming Services (Unity Cloud Save) with your guest account's player ID; only the developer sees it, and it is used to improve the game. The store version has no recording. If someone else is on the screen or can be heard, get their consent.
  • When you contact us: If you write to yigitkumral@icloud.com, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use them only to answer you and to fix the bug you report.
  • Permissions: On iOS the game may ask only for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency): in the consent regions only if you allow personalised ads on the game's screen, in other regions the first time you tap a rewarded ad button. It does not ask for microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos. Only in the TestFlight test version, if you start a recording, iOS asks for the microphone; if you do not allow it, the recording goes on without sound.

3. Who do we share data with?

We do not sell data. We share it only with these services so that the game works:

  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies): guest account, friends, race rooms, race results, crash reports, usage data (in the consent regions only if you allowed it) and the gameplay recordings you send from the TestFlight test version. While providing these services Unity derives your approximate region from your IP address (to pick a server); this does not depend on your ad or usage-data choice. Unity processes this data on our behalf and under our instructions. Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource): ad mediation. It is an independent controller under its own policy. Policy: https://developers.ironsrc.com/ironsource-mobile/air/ironsource-mobile-privacy-policy/
  • Unity Ads: ad network. It is an independent controller under its own policy. Policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Apple: App Store download, purchases and the tracking permission. Apple processes this data under its own policy. Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Other players: your username and race progress are visible to the players you race; your online status is visible to your friends. Your friend-room race result is visible to the players you raced in that room (section 2).
  • Legal requirements: we may share the necessary data if the law or a competent authority requires it.

4. Legal basis

  • Contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)): providing the game, the guest account, multiplayer races, friends, delivering purchased packs and account deletion.
  • Consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)): personalised ads and the iOS tracking permission in every region; usage data in the consent regions; the test feedback recordings you agree to before each recording in the TestFlight test version. You can withdraw your consent at any time (section 7; for test recordings, section 9).
  • Legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): showing and measuring non-personalised ads, preventing fraud, fixing bugs with crash reports, improving the game with usage data in other regions and answering support emails. You can object to usage data at any time and stop sending it with the switch in Settings.
  • Legal obligation (GDPR Article 6(1)(c)): answering rights requests and keeping records the law requires.

In Türkiye, Law No. 6698 (KVKK) applies, and Türkiye is one of the consent regions. Usage data, personalised ads and test feedback recordings are processed only with your explicit consent (Article 5/1). The guest account, races, friends and purchases rely on the formation and performance of a contract (Article 5/2(c)), crash reports and support emails on legitimate interests (Article 5/2(f)), and rights requests on legal obligation (Article 5/2(ç)). For transfers abroad (KVKK Article 9), the transfer for processing based on your explicit consent also relies on that consent; the other transfers the game needs to work take place with the standard contract safeguard that KVKK provides.

5. International transfers

Unity and Apple may process data outside your country, including in the United States. According to Unity's policy, transfers from the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom use standard contractual clauses. The basis for transfers from Türkiye is in section 4.

6. How long do we keep data?

  • Game data on your device: until you delete the app.
  • Guest account, username and friend list: until you delete your account.
  • Race results: until you delete your account.
  • Race room data: until the room closes.
  • Usage data: according to Unity's documentation, kept for 13 months and then deleted; if you delete your account, a deletion request is sent at once.
  • Crash reports: according to Unity's documentation, kept for 90 days and then deleted.
  • Test feedback recordings: at most 1 year after they are sent; we delete expired ones regularly. They are deleted sooner if you delete your account or write to us.
  • Support emails: 12 months after your request is closed; we delete them sooner if you ask. If a law (for example tax or commercial record rules) requires us to keep them longer, we keep only the part needed and only for that period.
  • Ad data: under the retention rules of Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads.

7. Your choices

The game asks you nothing when it opens. Some permissions work differently depending on your region.

  • Consent regions: the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Türkiye and devices whose region cannot be read.
  • Other regions: all other countries.

The game learns your region from your device's region setting; it does not use your location or IP address for this.

Consent regions Other regions

iOS tracking permission (ATT) iOS asks right after, only if you choose "Allow" on the game's screen. If you choose "Don't allow", it is not asked. iOS asks the first time you tap a rewarded ad button.

  • Personalised ads: In the consent regions you can change your answer at any time on the Settings > Privacy options screen. If you do not allow them, ads are shown without personalisation.
  • Opt-out of sale and sharing: In every region, the app tells the ad services not to sell or share your personal information (for US state laws). It also tells them that the game is not directed at children.

  • Playing without ads: Watching ads is never required; you can keep playing without watching any.

8. Your rights and how to use them

Depending on where you live, you have these rights: to access your data and get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict processing, to object, to data portability and to withdraw consent. In Türkiye, the rights in KVKK Article 11 also apply. In US states such as California, you have the right to know which data is collected, to delete it and to opt out of its sale or sharing.

  • Inside the app: change permissions in Settings; delete your account with Settings > Account > Delete account.
  • By email: write to yigitkumral@icloud.com. Because we hold no identity details, we may ask for your player name or the details on the Account screen to match your request to the right account. We reply within 30 days at the latest.
  • For ad data: you can also contact Unity directly; the options are in Unity's policy.
  • Complaints: If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority in your country (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Board).

9. Deleting your account

  1. Open Settings in the game.
  2. Tap the Account row.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Yes, delete.
  4. Stay connected to the internet. Deletion takes a few seconds.

Deleted: your guest account and player ID (including the server record), your name, your friend code, your friend list, your match history, your race results on the server and the gameplay recordings you sent from the TestFlight test version (including any still waiting on your phone). Unity Analytics is also asked to delete your usage data. Kept: your level progress, settings and powers stay on your device. Deleting does not refund purchases; refund requests go through Apple. If there is no internet or an error happens, nothing is deleted and you can try again. If you open multiplayer again, a new guest account is created for you.

If there is no Account row in Settings, you do not have a multiplayer account yet. If you cannot use the app, or you want to request deletion by email, write to yigitkumral@icloud.com with the subject "account deletion".

10. Children

The game is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. In the European Union, if you are under 16 (13–15 in some countries), you may allow usage data and personalised ads only with your parent's consent; if you are not sure, do not allow them. If you believe your child's data was collected, write to yigitkumral@icloud.com; we will delete it.

11. Security

According to Unity's documentation, data sent to Unity services travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Only the session on your device can access your account. No system is completely secure; if a breach happens, we will report it as the law requires.
