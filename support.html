Privacy policy

Restore purchases: Use "Restore purchases" in the app; consumed products are not granted again.

Delete account: While online, use Settings > Account > Delete account; contact us if you do not see the Account row or cannot access the app.

Permission choices: Usage data and Personalised ads are turned on or off independently in Settings; details are in section 7 of the privacy policy.

Test recording (TestFlight test version only): Settings > Account > Delete account deletes uploaded recordings and those still waiting on your phone. To request deletion by email, use the subject "test recording deletion" and include the recording's date and your device model.
