For questions, bug reports, suggestions and data requests: yigitkumral@icloud.com

If you are reporting a bug, it helps if you include: your device model, iOS version, the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings), which level you were on and what happened. If you attach a screenshot, make sure it contains no personal information. We reply as soon as we can.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Tap the arrows on the shape. An arrow slides in the direction its head points; if the way is clear, it leaves the shape. If another piece is in the way, it turns red and you lose a life. Drag with your finger to rotate the shape. The level is complete when all pieces are gone.

How do I restore a pack I bought?
Open the Sales Station and tap the "Restore purchases" button near the bottom. This button brings back orders that were paid for but not yet delivered to the game. Packs are consumable products: powers or +2 life tokens that were delivered and used are not granted again. If you paid and the pack did not arrive, close and reopen the app; if the problem continues, write to yigitkumral@icloud.com with the purchase date and the product.

My progress or powers are gone. Can you restore them?
Your level progress, powers and purchased packs are stored only on your device. There is no cloud save, so there is no copy on our server. Deleting the app or moving to a new device resets this data, and we cannot restore it. Refund requests go through Apple.

How do I delete my account?
Settings > Account > Delete account > Yes, delete. You need an internet connection; it takes a few seconds. Your guest account, name, friend code, friend list, match history and race results on the server are deleted, and a request to delete your usage data is sent. Your level progress, settings and powers stay on your device. If there is no Account row, you do not have a multiplayer account yet. To request deletion by email, write to yigitkumral@icloud.com with the subject "account deletion". Section 9 of the privacy policy has the details.

No ad is loading or I did not get my reward. What should I do?
- Check your internet connection; ads need a connection. - An ad may not be available at every moment; wait a little and try again. - Ad blockers, private DNS or VPN services can stop ads from loading. - Close and reopen the app. - The reward is given only if the ad is watched to the end; closing it early gives no reward.

If the problem continues, email yigitkumral@icloud.com with your device model and the time it happened.

How do I change my ad preferences?
In the European Union, the EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Türkiye (or if your device's region cannot be read), the game asks for personalised ads permission at your first rewarded ad; you can change your answer on the Settings > Privacy options screen. If you allow them there, iOS then asks for tracking permission. In other countries, iOS asks for tracking permission at your first rewarded ad. You can change your tracking answer on your iPhone in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Your answer never locks any feature; the ad reward is the same. Section 7 of the privacy policy has the details.

How do I turn off usage data?
You can turn it off at any time with the Settings > Usage data switch. In the European Union, the EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Türkiye (or if your device's region cannot be read), usage data starts off and you are asked once at the end of level 1; in other countries it starts on and you are told so at first launch.

How do I request a copy or deletion of my data?
Write to yigitkumral@icloud.com. To match your request to the right account, we may ask for your player name or the details on the Account screen. We reply within 30 days at the latest. For ad data you can also contact Unity directly; the links are in section 3 of the privacy policy.

Is the game free?
The game is free. The Sales Station sells optional power and +2 life packs. You can also earn powers with rewarded ads; watching ads is never required.

Which languages are supported?
Turkish, English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Indonesian, Hindi, Bengali, Arabic, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, German, Italian, Japanese and Korean.
